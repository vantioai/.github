# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Vantio’s public GitHub spaces a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the public Optics community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **hello@vantio.ai**. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently contributors for other behaviors they deem inappropriate.

We do not promise a response time for enforcement reports.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within public project spaces under `vantioai` and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or community.
